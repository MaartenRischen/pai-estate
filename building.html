As it is now

The building today

A large circular three-storey building. The structure is built, the interior is not finished. The first floor has a big open-air garden room, the second floor has living spaces. It was originally designed as a penthouse, and in 2023 acoustic drawings were made for a recording studio on one floor.

It stands in the forest, close to the river. The lower floors look into the trees and the top floor looks out over the valley.
